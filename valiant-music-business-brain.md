## H — Here's Who I Am

### My website
https://valiantmusic.com.au

### What inspired me, and my why
I believe in the power of good music — from all physical media to great gear and real
instruments — and in music's ability, in all its formats, to change lives on a daily basis.
Great gear and tone, and inspiring instruments, make great music and make people smile, move,
scream, weep and love — a physical embodiment of what it means to be truly alive.

And all those artefacts of days gone by that elicit the pure homecoming of enamoured nostalgia
deserve to be maintained and fixed, to bring that light into someone's life. So repairs and
maintenance of guitars, music gear and hi-fi equipment is a core focus too.

### Who I help
The people who come in on holidays and buy a $5k guitar in 10 minutes. Moving high-end
consignments at scale would help significantly.

### What the business actually does
1. **Curated vintage & collectable guitars** — acoustic, electric and bass, from celebrated
   brands, spanning price points. Genuine collectables with history (e.g. a 1965 Epiphone
   FT-45 Cortez, a 2004 Fender Custom Shop Stratocaster, a Guild X160 Rockabilly).
2. **Amplifiers & effects pedals** — boutique and classic (Orange, Boss, MXR, Walrus Audio,
   EarthQuaker Devices, TC Electronic, Electro-Harmonix, Dunlop and more).
3. **Physical media & hi-fi** — vinyl records, CDs, tapes, MiniDisc (even pianola rolls),
   plus classic stereo/hi-fi equipment (turntables, styli, speakers). "From rare jazz and
   soul gems to iconic rock and pop collectables."
4. **Expert repairs, setups & maintenance** — restrings, full professional setups, major
   repairs, restorations, fine-tuning to a player's exact style, plus valuations and help
   **selling** your own gear.
5. **Accessories & essentials** — strings, stands, capos, picks, straps, cables, tuners,
   drumsticks, harmonicas, microphones and more. "Tested by musicians, not marketing teams."

*Secondary / legacy:* **gear and backline hire** (amplifiers, keyboards, stands, drums and
hardware for gigs, touring and studio sessions). Available but not a current marketing focus.
